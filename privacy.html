🐰 Mai Sakurajima: Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Mai Sakurajima ("Mai", "the bot") is a Discord bot for gaming and chill communities. This policy explains what Mai stores, why, and how you can see or delete it. By using Mai in a server, you agree to this policy.

What Mai stores

Mai only stores what her features need. Data is stored per server, linked to your Discord user ID.

Message content

Mai reads messages in channels she can see. She does this for automod (spam, links, bad words), auto-responders, AFK mentions and anti-nuke protection, and to count messages for leveling. She does not keep a copy of your messages, with two exceptions that a server's staff turn on:

  • Moderation logs: deleted or edited messages, and messages removed by automod, are posted to that server's private log channel.
  • Ticket transcripts: when a support ticket is closed, its messages are saved as a text file in that server's log channel.

These logs live in Discord channels owned by the server, not in Mai's own storage.

Services Mai talks to

Some commands send what you type to outside services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Music: song searches and links go to YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify. Lyrics come from lrclib.net.
  • /lookup: text you translate goes to Google Translate, city names go to Open-Meteo, and words go to Urban Dictionary.
  • Roleplay GIFs come from nekos.best.

Mai never sends your Discord ID or account details to these services.

Where it's kept and who can see it

  • Data is stored on a private server run by Mai's owner. It is never sold, rented or shared for advertising.
  • Leaderboards, levels, petals and invite counts are visible to other members of the same server. Warnings are visible to that server's staff.
  • Mai's owner can access stored data to run and fix the bot.

How long it's kept

Data is kept while Mai is in the server, so your levels and stats aren't lost. Daily and weekly stats reset automatically. You can delete your own data at any time (see below). A server's admins can reset stats or settings with Mai's admin commands.

Your choices

  • /mydata view shows what Mai stores about you in that server.
  • /mydata delete deletes it. Warnings and jail records are kept, because they belong to the server's moderation history. Ask that server's staff to remove them.
  • To have your data removed from every server, or for any other question, contact Mai's owner (below).

Children

Mai is meant for people allowed to use Discord under Discord's own Terms of Service. She doesn't knowingly collect extra data about anyone under that age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Continuing to use Mai after a change means you accept it.
